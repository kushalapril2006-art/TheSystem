Journal Fraktur is a renamed, subsetted derivative of Latin Modern Math 1.959,
by Boguslaw Jackowski, Piotr Strzelczyk and Piotr Pianowski.
Its bold Fraktur glyphs are mapped to regular Latin codepoints for headings.
The derived font is embedded in fonts.css. build-font-css.py contains the
changes and can regenerate it from the original OpenType font files.
Upstream source and notices: https://ctan.org/pkg/lm-math

Journal Roman is a CSS family alias for the unchanged Latin Modern Roman
10 regular and bold OpenType faces, embedded in fonts.css.
Upstream source and notices: https://ctan.org/pkg/lm
